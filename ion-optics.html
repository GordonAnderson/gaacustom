Ion Optics Component

RF/DC Ion Funnel

A PCB-based ion funnel for focusing and transporting ions from a higher-pressure region into vacuum. Gold-plated ring electrodes are driven with an RF confinement field superimposed on a linear DC axial gradient, substantially improving ion transmission compared to a simple orifice or skimmer. The all-PCB construction is robust and easily cleaned in a sonicator, and the design is built to be driven directly by GAACE RF Generator or RF Mega drivers.

Ion Optics Component

PCB KF Vacuum Feedthroughs

Low-cost, PCB-based vacuum feedthroughs for bringing electrical signals into a vacuum chamber — an economical alternative to traditional machined feedthroughs. Available in KF16, KF25, and KF40 flange sizes with a wide range of connector options, each feedthrough is tested to 10⁻⁶ Torr.

The D9 variant brings a 9-pin D-sub connector on the atmosphere side down to pin connectors on the vacuum side — vacuum-side wiring with socketed PTFE leads makes pin assignments easy to change as your setup evolves.
